Reading notes

  • Compliance = 100 - 100 x weighted errors / weighted checks (pass + error), weights high 3, medium 2, low 1, info excluded. Excellent ≥ 90, good ≥ 60, average ≥ 40, critical below.
  • Errors / device = error lines of risk high, medium and low divided by the number of devices in scope.
  • Devices affected = devices with at least one error, warning or vulnerable PSIRT on the test, whatever the filters.
  • PSIRT: only the vulnerable results are counted as PSIRT; a device that is not affected or whose configuration was not found is shown as «not affected».
  • This report only holds counters: no configuration line, line number or device name is embedded.
